💡 Knowing the syntax is only the beginning. The follow-up is where interviews get interesting.

A simple practice routine:

1. How does ConcurrentHashMap achieve concurrency without locking the whole map?
2. What happens when Thread.stop is used and why is it unsafe?
3. What is Exchanger and where could it be used?
4. ArrayBlockingQueue vs LinkedBlockingQueue: what trade-offs matter?
5. volatile vs synchronized: what problem does each solve?

Now explain this one with a real production example:
6. How would you scan uploaded files for malware safely?

📌 A strong interview answer usually covers: what it is, why it exists, when to use it and what can go wrong.
