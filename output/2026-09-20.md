What I would practice before the interview:

1. What does unordered() do to a stream?
2. How would you group transactions by month using Streams?
3. What exception occurs when a stream is reused?
4. When can unordered streams improve parallel performance?
5. How does limit() behave on ordered parallel streams?

🚨 EXPECT A FOLLOW-UP ON THIS
6. How would you detect a Redis hot key?

📌 For senior roles, connect every answer to scalability, reliability, observability and the decision you would make.
