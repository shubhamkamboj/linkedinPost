🏗️ System design interviews are less about drawing boxes and more about explaining trade-offs.

📝 INTERVIEW CHECKLIST

1. Design a distributed lock for scheduled jobs across multiple nodes.
2. Design a system that protects a database during sudden traffic spikes.
3. Design a service that provides consistent configuration to thousands of instances.
4. Design a coupon service that prevents double redemption.
5. Design a returns workflow that updates inventory and refunds without conflicting updates.

🔥 Then prepare for this:
6. What happens when the database connection pool is exhausted?

📌 Do not memorize the answer. Explain the concept, the trade-off, the failure mode and a real production use case.
